# Benchmark: ideas for later

Decided with the owner on 2026-11-21 but not built yet. Revisit them together before starting.

## Levels 4, 5 and 6

- For advanced members. Nothing about them shows until a member reaches Level 3 on that test.
- Unlocking: after 3 benchmarks in a row at Level 3, a member can move on to Level 4 and up.
- Known targets so far:
  - **Broad jump:** Level 4 = height + 3 ft, Level 5 = height + 6 ft. The jump from +3 to +6 ft is big; revisit before building.
  - **Hand-release push-ups:** Level 4 = 50, Level 5 = 60, Level 6 = 70 reps.
  - Every other test still needs its Level 4–6 targets.
- Building this changes scoring (max score, overall level, the "Keep Level 3" plan), so it needs a new benchmark version once v1 is frozen.

## Equipment

- Standard gym equipment is the baseline for every test and workout.
- Later: show which equipment each exercise needs.

## Calendar and workout days

- A calendar showing the Sunday runs every week. The runs already repeat weekly through event series; this is about a calendar view.
- Members pick the days they're free to train, and the three workouts fill those days on the calendar.
